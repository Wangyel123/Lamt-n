# Questions for the challenge channel (Phase 0)

Ask these between 08:00 and 20:00. Each one shows the reviewers a gap or ambiguity I spotted.

1. **"<5" in AC-05: group size or cell count?** The brief says "any group with fewer than 5 students shown as <5". Does "group" mean a class with under 5 students, or any single count (e.g. 2 Urgent in a class of 12) under 5? A count of 1–4 Urgent students in a known class could still identify a child. I plan to take the stricter reading unless told otherwise.
2. **Back-calculation:** School D has only 8 students (XI = 2, XII = 6). If XI is hidden but the School D total is shown, XI can be worked out by subtraction. Should supervisors see school totals at all, or should I apply complementary suppression?
3. **"Prefer not to say" on the self-harm question:** under the baseline rules, 7 of the 8 students who chose this end up with no flag. Is it intended that a skipped self-harm answer is treated as "No"? I propose raising it to at least High (not Urgent) and showing the reason.
4. **Missing or messy answers:** one student has a blank sleep answer and one wrote "often" in lowercase. Should I treat blanks as "missing, needs checking" (my plan) rather than as "fine", and normalise case/spacing?
5. **Free-text comments:** the baseline rules ignore comments, but one says "Sometimes I don't want to go home". May I add a simple keyword screen (clearly labelled as crude) that raises such students to Review?
6. **Counsellor scope:** in the real platform, does each counsellor see only their own school? For the demo I plan one counsellor account that sees all four schools, clearly labelled demo-only.
7. **Supervisor level:** is the supervisor a CECD (national) role seeing all schools, or a Dzongkhag-level role seeing a subset?
8. **Who can close an Urgent case?** Should closing an Urgent case require a note (my plan), or a second person's sign-off?
9. **Helpline:** is there an official helpline or referral contact CECD wants shown to students and counsellors? I will not invent a number.
