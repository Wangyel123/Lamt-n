# Draft: email to the CECD Director (rewrite in your own words)

**Subject:** A first tool to help counsellors reach students sooner

Dear Director,

I have built the first small piece of the wellbeing platform: a simple screen that helps school counsellors decide which students to meet first after the Situational Analysis survey.

It reads each student's answers and puts them into four groups, from Urgent to No concerns, and explains in plain words why. Urgent students always appear at the top. Counsellors can record each meeting, and every change is kept. It works on phones as well as computers.

I also found a gap: students who chose "prefer not to say" on the self-harm question were being missed. The tool now asks counsellors to check in with them gently.

Supervisors see only numbers for each school. No names appear, and small groups are hidden so no child can be identified.

I used made-up data only. I would welcome the chance to test it with two or three counsellors.
